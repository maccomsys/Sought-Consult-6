Sought Consult Delivers Reliable Construction and Property Solutions

Based at Oklemekuku Hotel, Teshei in Greater Accra, Sought Consult is a Ghanaian construction and property solutions company providing civil works, building construction, fine and coarse aggregate supply, land sales, and architectural and structural drawings.

From initial design plans and verified land acquisition to quality river sand, filling sand, and quarry stone deliveries via 16, 18, and 20 cubic tipper trucks, we support individuals, businesses, and developers across Ghana with dependable execution.

PREMIUM BUILDING MATERIALS

Quality Aggregates & Construction Sands

Order top-grade quarry stones, riversand, filling sand, and plastering sand delivered directly to your site via 16, 18, and 20 cubic tipper trucks. Select any material below to request a quote.

Quarry Stones / Chippings

Durable crushed granite stones in 3/4", 1", 3/8", and 5/8" sizes for concrete casting and slab works.

Riversand

Clean, washed river sand (smooth, medium, and rough) ideal for high-strength mortar, bricklaying, and plastering.

Filling Sand

Grade 1 filling sand and laterite for foundational backfilling, leveling, and earthworks compaction.

Sand / Plastering Sand

Smooth, medium, and rough construction/plastering sands screened for pristine wall finishes and brickwork.
